Questions

Before you ask

How long does a website take?

A one-page site is typically 5 working days from the day I receive your text and images. A five-page site is around 10 working days. I give you a real date before we start.

Do I need to write the content myself?

No. Send your existing material — brochures, WhatsApp messages, anything — and I will structure the copy. I do not invent claims about your business; anything I write, you approve.

What do I actually receive?

The full source files, a deployed site, and the domain/hosting account in your name. Nothing is locked to me — if you ever want another developer, everything is yours.

How does payment work?

50% upfront to book the slot, 50% when the site is live and you are happy. Bank transfer, Wise, or any method that works in your country.

Can you maintain the site afterwards?

Yes, optionally. Small text and image updates are billed as a flat monthly retainer, or per-update if you prefer. You are never forced into it.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes — most of the work is remote and async. Calls are scheduled in your time zone.
